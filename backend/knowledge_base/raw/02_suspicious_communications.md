# Handling suspicious calls, SMS and messaging-app communications
Source URL: https://sancharsaathi.gov.in/
Topic: suspicious_communication_reporting

This CyberSaathi knowledge note is a concise operational summary, not an official government publication. Verify current reporting options and eligibility on the linked official service before production use.

For a suspicious call, SMS or messaging-app communication, do not rely on caller ID, a displayed logo, a screenshot, or a caller's claimed job title as proof of identity. Verify independently using contact details obtained from the organisation's official website, app, card or statement.

Do not follow payment instructions from an unverified caller. Do not scan a QR code, approve a UPI collect request, install a remote-access application, open an unexpected attachment, or share OTPs, PINs, passwords, CVV or recovery codes.

Chakshu, available through Sanchar Saathi, is a reporting facility for suspected fraudulent communications. It is not a substitute for filing a cybercrime complaint when a person has actually lost money. For an actual financial cyber-fraud loss, the person should contact their bank immediately, call 1930 promptly and use the National Cyber Crime Reporting Portal.

Preserve the original message, sender identifier, call details, timestamp, URL and screenshots where safe. Avoid forwarding suspicious links to other people.
