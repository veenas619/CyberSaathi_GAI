# Responding to suspected financial cyber fraud in India
Source URL: https://cybercrime.gov.in/
Topic: financial_cyber_fraud_reporting

This CyberSaathi knowledge note is a concise operational summary. It is not an official circular and must be checked against the linked government portal before production use.

When a person reports money actually debited or lost through suspected financial cyber fraud, advise them to contact their bank immediately using a phone number or contact route obtained independently from the bank's official app, website, card or statement. Ask the bank to record the complaint and explain protective steps available for the account and transaction.

For suspected financial cyber fraud in India, the national cyber-fraud helpline is 1930. The complainant should report promptly. The National Cyber Crime Reporting Portal is https://cybercrime.gov.in/. Preserve the complaint acknowledgement or reference number.

Preserve relevant evidence: transaction reference or UTR, amount, date and time, bank alerts, screenshots, messages, call details, phone numbers, URLs and app names. Do not edit or delete the original evidence where it can be retained safely.

Do not promise that money will be recovered. Reporting quickly does not guarantee recovery. Do not ask the user to disclose an OTP, UPI PIN, password, CVV, full card number or full banking credentials to CyberSaathi.

A suspicious call or message without a confirmed debit is not the same as a confirmed financial loss. Establish whether the user actually authorised or made a payment, whether a debit is visible in their own bank statement, and whether credentials or remote access were shared. Do not delay urgent bank contact when the user clearly reports an unauthorised debit.
