# Initial response to suspected account or device compromise
Source URL: https://www.cert-in.org.in/
Topic: account_device_security

This CyberSaathi knowledge note is general defensive guidance, not a CERT-In advisory. Check the linked official advisories for current technical recommendations.

If an account may be compromised, use a trusted device and the service's official application or website to begin account recovery. Change the affected password, enable multi-factor authentication where available, review active sessions and sign out sessions the user does not recognise. Do not reuse a compromised password on other services.

If a remote-access or screen-sharing application was installed at an unknown person's request, stop the session, disconnect the device from the network if remote control appears active, and seek help from a trusted technical support provider. Review installed applications and permissions. Do not enter banking credentials while an untrusted person may be viewing or controlling the device.

If banking details, an OTP, a UPI PIN or account access may have been exposed, contact the bank through an independently verified official channel immediately. Check for unauthorised transactions. If money has been lost through suspected cyber fraud in India, call 1930 promptly and report at https://cybercrime.gov.in/.

Do not assume that clicking a link alone proves an account or device was compromised. Ask whether the user entered information, downloaded or installed anything, granted permissions, approved a login, or observed unusual account activity.
