# UPI and digital payment safety
Source URL: https://www.npci.org.in/what-we-do/upi/product-overview
Topic: upi_payment_safety

This CyberSaathi knowledge note is a practical summary and is not an official NPCI circular. Verify current product instructions on the linked NPCI website and the user's bank or UPI app before production use.

A UPI PIN is used to authorise a payment. A user should not enter a UPI PIN merely to receive money. A QR code or collect request should not be treated as proof that money has been credited. The user should independently check the balance and transaction history in their own bank or trusted UPI application.

Do not approve an unexpected collect request or enter a PIN because a caller says it is needed to receive a refund, cancel a transaction, verify a credit or unlock an account. Do not send money to a different UPI ID just because an unknown caller claims to have made an accidental transfer.

If an unexpected credit is genuinely visible, do not spend or move it. Contact the bank through an independently verified official channel and ask it to investigate and advise on the proper handling of the credit.

If money has actually been debited through suspected fraud, contact the bank immediately, call 1930 promptly and file a report at https://cybercrime.gov.in/. Preserve the UPI transaction ID/UTR, timestamps and relevant messages. Never share a UPI PIN or OTP with CyberSaathi or another person.
