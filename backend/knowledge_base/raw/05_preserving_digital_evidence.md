# Preserving evidence after a suspected cyber incident
Source URL: https://cybercrime.gov.in/
Topic: evidence_preservation

This CyberSaathi knowledge note is general evidence-preservation guidance. Follow instructions from the bank or relevant authority for the specific case.

Keep original messages, emails, call logs, screenshots, transaction notifications, payment reference numbers, UTRs, dates, times, sender identifiers, phone numbers, URLs and relevant application names. Record a short chronological account of what happened while details are fresh.

Do not delete the original messages or reset a device merely to remove suspicious content before considering evidence-preservation needs. Do not interact further with a suspicious sender just to collect more evidence. Do not publish sensitive evidence publicly.

Never share OTPs, passwords, UPI PINs, CVVs, recovery codes or full account credentials with CyberSaathi. If a screenshot contains sensitive information, redact it before sharing with anyone who does not need the unredacted original.

For actual financial cyber-fraud loss in India, evidence collection must not delay contacting the bank, calling 1930 promptly and filing a report at https://cybercrime.gov.in/.
